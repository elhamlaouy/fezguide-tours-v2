Frequently Asked Questions
Planning a trip to Fez? Here are answers to some of the questions travellers often ask before booking a tour or experience with us.
About Our Tours
What types of experiences do you offer in Fez?
We offer private tours and local experiences in Fez, including Medina tours, food and tasting experiences, cultural experiences and customized visits. We also organize private day trips from Fez to destinations such as Chefchaouen, Meknes, Volubilis, Moulay Idriss and Ifrane.
Are your tours private?
Most of our experiences can be arranged privately, meaning the experience is for you and your group rather than a large group of other travellers. The exact format depends on the experience you choose.
Can I customize my tour?
Yes. If you have specific places you want to visit, particular interests or limited time, let us know before booking. We can suggest a practical route or adapt the experience when possible.
How long do your tours take?
The duration depends on the experience. Medina tours generally take a few hours, while food experiences and day trips have their own schedules. The exact duration is shown on each experience page.
Booking
How can I book?
You can book through the booking option available on the experience page or contact us directly if you have questions before booking.
Can I book at short notice?
We will always try to accommodate last-minute requests, depending on guide and transportation availability. For the best choice of time and guide, we recommend booking in advance.
What happens after I book?
After your booking is confirmed, we will provide the important details you need, including the meeting point or pickup arrangements, time and any other information specific to your experience.
Can I change my booking?
In many cases, we can help change the date or time if we are informed early enough and availability allows it. Please contact us as soon as possible if your plans change.
Our Guides
Are your guides local?
Yes. We work with local guides who know Fez, its history, culture, Medina and everyday life.
What languages do your guides speak?
Depending on availability, our guides can provide experiences in English, French and Spanish. If you need another language, contact us before booking and we will check what is possible.
Will I have a private guide?
For private experiences, you will have a guide dedicated to you and your group. This allows you to ask questions, take your time and have a more personal experience.
Meeting Points & Transportation
Where do we meet for a Medina tour?
The meeting point depends on the experience and your location. Common meeting points include Bab Boujloud and other accessible points around the Old Medina.
We will confirm the exact meeting point with you before the tour.
Can you pick us up from our riad?
This depends on where your accommodation is located.
Cars cannot enter the narrow pedestrian streets of the Old Medina. If your riad is inside the Medina, we can arrange a convenient meeting point that is accessible by foot or help organize transportation to a suitable pickup point.
Do you provide transportation for day trips?
Yes. Private transportation can be arranged for our day trips from Fez. The vehicle and driver arrangements depend on the experience you choose.
Walking in the Medina
Is the Old Medina difficult to walk around?
The Medina involves quite a lot of walking, and some streets are narrow, uneven or include steps and slopes.
You do not need to be an experienced hiker, but comfortable shoes are strongly recommended.
Are your tours suitable for older travellers?
Many of our experiences can be adapted to your pace. If you have difficulty walking or need a less demanding route, please tell us before booking so we can suggest a suitable option.
Can we take breaks during the tour?
Of course. A private tour gives you more flexibility, so you can stop for photos, Moroccan tea, food or a short rest when needed.
Families & Children
Are children welcome?
Yes. Families are welcome on our experiences.
Do children pay the same price?
This depends on the experience and the age of the child. Some experiences may have special arrangements for children. Contact us with the ages of your children and we can confirm the details.
Food Experiences
What food will we taste?
The food depends on the experience and what is available locally on the day. Our food experiences can include traditional Fassi and Moroccan foods, snacks, sweets, tea and other local specialities.
Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Please tell us about any allergies, dietary restrictions or foods you cannot eat when booking. We will do our best to adapt the experience when possible.
Is food included in the food tour?
Yes. Food and tastings are included according to the specific experience you book. Please check the experience page for the exact inclusions.
Day Trips from Fez
Which destinations can I visit from Fez?
We organize private day trips to destinations including Chefchaouen, Meknes, Volubilis, Moulay Idriss and Ifrane and the Middle Atlas.
We can also help create a customized day trip based on your interests and available time.
Are day trips private?
Our day trips are designed as private experiences for you and your group.
Can I choose how we spend the day?
Yes. We can suggest a practical itinerary, but we understand that every traveller has different interests. When possible, we can adapt the route and the amount of time spent at each destination.
Are entrance fees included?
Entrance fees depend on the experience. Any entrance tickets that are not included will be clearly mentioned in the experience description.
Prices & What Is Included
What is included in the price?
Each experience is different. The experience page will explain what is included and what is not included, such as guides, transportation, food or entrance tickets.
Are tips included?
Tips are generally not included. They are always at your discretion and should reflect your satisfaction with the service.
Do I need to carry cash?
It is useful to have some Moroccan dirhams with you, especially for small purchases, local food, taxis and places that may not accept cards.
Changes & Cancellations
What is your cancellation policy?
The cancellation conditions depend on the experience and booking platform. Please check the cancellation policy shown when you make your booking.
What happens if I am late?
If you think you are going to be late, please contact us as soon as possible. We will do our best to coordinate with you and your guide or driver.
What happens if the weather affects our experience?
If weather conditions make an experience unsafe or impractical, we will discuss the available options with you, which may include changing the schedule or adapting the experience.
Still Have a Question?
If you cannot find the answer you are looking for, feel free to contact us.
We are happy to answer your questions and help you choose the right experience for your time in Fez.
