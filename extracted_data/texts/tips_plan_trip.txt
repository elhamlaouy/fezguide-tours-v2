Planning Your Visit to Fez


  



Planning a trip to Fez does not have to be complicated.


The city has a lot to offer, from the historic Medina and its traditional crafts to the Mellah, local food, viewpoints and day trips into the surrounding countryside.


The best way to enjoy it is to give yourself enough time to explore without trying to see everything in one day.


How Many Days Do You Need in Fez?


  



For a first visit, we recommend spending at least 2 to 3 days in Fez.


This gives you enough time to explore the Old Medina, discover its historical landmarks, experience local food and still have time to see another side of the city.


If you have more time, 4 days or longer allows you to slow down and include a day trip from Fez, such as Chefchaouen, Meknes and Volubilis, or the Middle Atlas.


Your ideal length of stay depends on what you want to experience.


When Is the Best Time to Visit Fez?


  



Fez can be visited throughout the year, but spring and autumn are particularly comfortable for spending long hours walking around the Medina.


The weather is generally milder, making it easier to explore on foot.


Summer can be hot, especially during the middle of the day, while winter can bring cooler mornings and evenings.


If you are mainly interested in walking, sightseeing and exploring the Medina, choosing a comfortable season can make a noticeable difference.


Plan Your Medina Visit


  



The Old Medina deserves time.


Do not plan to visit several major attractions in a few hours and expect to understand the city.


Give yourself time to walk through the streets, stop at the souks, visit historical sites, discover traditional workshops and simply observe everyday life.


A private local guide can also help you organise your time around the places you are most interested in.


Choose Where You Stay Carefully


  



Fez has accommodation in different parts of the city.


Staying inside or close to the Old Medina gives you easy access to the historic streets and allows you to experience the atmosphere of the old city.


Staying in the New City can be more convenient if you prefer modern streets, easier vehicle access and a different side of Fez.


Neither option is necessarily better.


It depends on the kind of experience you want.


One important thing to remember: cars cannot normally enter the narrow streets of the Old Medina, so if your accommodation is inside the Medina, you may need to walk from the nearest accessible point with your luggage.


Think About What You Want to Experience


  



Before arriving, think about what interests you most.


Do you want to focus on:


- History and monuments?
- Traditional crafts and souks?
- Moroccan and Fassi food?
- Local life and culture?
- Photography and viewpoints?
- A relaxed private walking experience?
- Day trips outside Fez?


Knowing what you want helps you build a better itinerary instead of simply following a list of tourist attractions.


Leave Some Time for the Unexpected


One of the best things about Fez is that not everything needs to be planned.


Leave some free time in your schedule.


You may find a small food shop you want to try, discover a traditional workshop, stop for Moroccan tea or find a viewpoint you did not know about.


The Medina is full of small details that are easy to miss when you are rushing from one place to another.


Planning a Day Trip from Fez


  



Fez is also a great base for exploring other parts of northern and central Morocco.


You can take a private day trip to places such as:


Chefchaouen — famous for its blue streets, old town and mountain setting.


Meknes, Volubilis & Moulay Idriss — a combination of imperial history, Roman ruins and a traditional pilgrimage town.


Ifrane & the Middle Atlas — a different side of Morocco, with forests, mountains and countryside.


Private day trips give you more flexibility, allowing you to spend more time at the places that interest you rather than following a rushed schedule.


Plan, But Don't Overplan


  



A good trip to Fez needs a plan, but it also needs space.


Book the important things in advance, especially accommodation and experiences you really want to do.


Then leave some room to discover the city naturally.


Fez is not a city you fully understand by checking attractions off a list.


It is a city you experience by walking, tasting, observing and taking your time.
