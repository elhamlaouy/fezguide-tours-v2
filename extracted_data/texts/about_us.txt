About Us & Our Team
About Us
We are a small local team based in Fez.
We created Fez Guide Tours to share our city with travellers and help them discover Fez in a simple, comfortable and personal way.
We know the streets of the Medina, the different areas of the city, the local food, traditions and everyday life.
Our goal is simple: to help you enjoy Fez and discover the city through local eyes.

Meet Our Team
Med
Founder & Local Host

Our Local Guides
We work with local guides who know Fez, its history, culture and Medina.
They are here to share their knowledge, answer your questions and help you enjoy your time in the city.
  
  
  
  

Our Drivers & Local Partners
For day trips, transfers and other experiences, we work with trusted local drivers and partners.
They help us make your experience comfortable, organized and enjoyable.
  
  
  
  
  

Why We Do It
We believe that the best way to discover Fez is not only by visiting its famous places, but also by seeing how the city really lives.
We want to welcome you to our city, share what we know and help you create good memories in Fez.
Welcome to Fez. See the city through local eyes.
