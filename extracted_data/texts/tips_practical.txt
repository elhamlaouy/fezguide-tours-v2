A few simple things can make your stay in Fez much easier and more comfortable.


Fez is a historic city with its own rhythm and customs. You do not need to know everything before you arrive. But knowing a few practical details can help you feel more comfortable, avoid common misunderstandings and enjoy your time in the city.


  



Carry Some Cash


It is useful to have some Moroccan dirhams with you during your stay.


Cards are accepted in many hotels, restaurants and modern shops, but cash is still important for small purchases, local food, taxis, small shops and traditional markets.


It is better to keep some smaller notes and coins with you, especially when spending time in the Medina.


Wear Comfortable Shoes


This may sound simple, but comfortable shoes can make a big difference in Fez.


You will probably walk more than you expect, especially when exploring the Old Medina. The streets can be uneven, and some areas have steps, slopes and narrow passages.


Choose comfortable shoes that you can walk in for several hours.


Dress Comfortably and Respectfully


Fez is a traditional Moroccan city, and modest, comfortable clothing is generally the easiest choice.


You do not need special clothing, but covering the shoulders and knees is a good way to respect the local environment, particularly when visiting religious or traditional places.


The most important thing is to feel comfortable while also being respectful of the people and places around you.


Keep Your Phone and Belongings Safe


As in any busy tourist destination, keep an eye on your personal belongings, especially in crowded markets and busy streets.


Keep your phone, wallet and important items somewhere secure and avoid leaving them unattended.


You can enjoy the Medina much more when you are not constantly worried about your belongings.


Getting a SIM Card and Mobile Data


Having mobile data can be very useful in Fez, especially if you are exploring independently.


It can help you contact your accommodation, use maps, communicate with your driver or guide and stay connected during your trip.


SIM cards are available in Morocco from different mobile operators. If you need help getting one, ask your accommodation or local contact where to buy one.


Check Opening Hours


Opening hours in Fez can vary from one place to another.


Museums, monuments, shops, restaurants and markets do not always follow exactly the same schedule, and opening times can change depending on the day or season.


If there is a particular place you really want to visit, it is a good idea to check its opening hours before going.


Friday Has a Different Rhythm


Friday is an important day in Morocco, and the rhythm of the city can be different.


Some businesses or places may have different opening hours, and some attractions can close or operate on a different schedule.


If you are planning to visit specific monuments, museums or traditional places on Friday, check in advance so you can organize your day accordingly.


Shopping in the Medina


Shopping in the Medina is part of the Fez experience.


You will find traditional crafts, leather goods, textiles, ceramics, zellige, metalwork, spices and many other local products.


Take your time before buying something. Compare products and prices, ask questions and make sure you understand what you are buying.


If you are not interested in buying something, a friendly “no, thank you” is usually enough. There is no need to feel pressured.


  



Food and Water


Fez has a rich food culture, and trying local food is one of the best ways to experience the city.


When choosing where to eat, look for places that are busy with local customers and have a good turnover of food.


For drinking water, bottled water is an easy option for visitors.


If you have a sensitive stomach or are not used to Moroccan food, start slowly and give yourself time to enjoy the different flavours.


Taking Photos


Fez is full of interesting places to photograph, from architecture and crafts to colourful markets and everyday street life.


But remember that people are part of that life too.


Before taking a close-up photo of someone, especially a shopkeeper, craftsman or local resident, it is respectful to ask first.


You may also find that some people do not want to be photographed. Simply respect their answer.


  



Take Breaks


Do not try to see everything in one day.


The Old Medina is large, and walking through it for several hours can be tiring, especially during warmer months.


Stop for a Moroccan tea, sit in a small café, enjoy a quiet courtyard or simply take a break and watch the city around you.


Sometimes the best moments in Fez happen when you are not rushing to the next attraction.


Be Flexible


Fez does not always work according to the same rhythm as a modern tourist destination.


A shop may be closed, a street may be busier than expected, or your plans may change during the day.


Instead of seeing this as a problem, leave some space in your schedule.


Be flexible, take your time and enjoy the experience. Some of the most memorable moments can come from things you did not plan.


A Few Local Tips Can Make a Big Difference


You do not need to know every rule or custom before visiting Fez.


A little patience, respect and curiosity will take you a long way.


If you are unsure about something, simply ask your hotel, guide or a trusted local contact. Most importantly, take your time and enjoy the city at its own rhythm.
